# Enclosure Design

## Overview

The LusoClip enclosure was designed as a slim, clip-on wearable that blends into everyday clothing. The form factor is inspired by professional lapel microphones to leverage existing social norms around wearable audio accessories.

The enclosure prioritizes discretion, comfort, and stability over visual expression or branding.

---

## Target Dimensions

- Approximate size: **55 mm × 32 mm × 9–11 mm**
- Weight target: **< 25 g**
- Profile: slim and wide to avoid fabric bulging or rotation

The slim profile was intentionally chosen to maintain an elegant appearance and ensure comfort during extended wear.

---

## Form Factor

- Soft rectangular “pill” shape
- Rounded edges and corners
- No front-facing logos or text
- Neutral color palette (charcoal, warm gray, deep navy)

From the front, the device should read as an accessory rather than a piece of electronics.

---

## Clip Mechanism

- Integrated rear spring clip
- Hidden spring assembly
- One-handed attach/remove
- Rubberized inner contact surface to prevent slipping

The clip positions the device close to the garment plane to minimize outward tilt and visual presence.

---

## Materials & Finish

- Shell: PC-ABS or similar durable polymer
- Finish: satin or soft-touch matte
- Fingerprint-resistant coating
- Internal standoffs to protect battery and electronics

Materials were selected to balance durability, weight, and manufacturability.

---

## Indicators & Feedback

- No visible LEDs during normal operation
- All operational feedback handled through the mobile app
- Optional hidden indicator during charging only

Visible indicators were intentionally removed to avoid making conversation partners feel observed or recorded.

---

## Internal Layout (Conceptual)

- Ultra-flat Li-Po battery positioned closest to garment-facing shell
- Microcontroller positioned toward top of enclosure
- MEMS microphone aligned with front-facing acoustic port
- USB-C charging port located on bottom edge

Battery placement prioritizes balance and wear stability.

---

## Design Rationale

The enclosure design reflects a broader product philosophy:  
**technology should support conversation without becoming the focus of it.**

By minimizing visual cues and interaction requirements, LusoClip allows multilingual conversations to remain natural and human-centered.
