# Privacy Model

## Design Intent

LusoClip was designed to support trust in multilingual interactions by minimizing visible monitoring cues and avoiding persistent audio storage.

---

## Privacy Guarantees

- No audio stored on the wearable device
- No persistent audio storage on the smartphone
- Audio discarded immediately after translation
- No visible recording indicators during use

---

## Trust-Centered Design

By eliminating storage and interaction cues, the system reduces the likelihood that conversation partners feel recorded or evaluated by technology.

Privacy is treated as a core design constraint rather than a secondary feature.
