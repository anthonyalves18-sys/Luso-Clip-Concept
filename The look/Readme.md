# Design

This section documents the physical and industrial design decisions behind **LusoClip**, with a focus on discretion, comfort, and social acceptability.

The goal of the hardware design is not to appear as a “translator device,” but rather as a neutral wearable accessory similar to a premium lapel microphone.

## Design Principles

- Minimal visual presence during conversation
- No visible recording indicators during use
- Slim, lightweight form factor suitable for daily wear
- Familiar clip-on interaction to reduce social friction
- Battery-powered, self-contained enclosure
